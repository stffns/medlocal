# CHT Workflow: Delivery

*Decision support workflow from Community Health Toolkit (CHT)*
*Used by 40,000+ community health workers in 15 countries*

## Danger Signs to Check
Ask about EACH of the following. If ANY is present, refer immediately.

- Postnatal Danger Sign Check - Woman
- Fever
- Severe headache
- Vaginal bleeding
- Foul smelling vaginal discharge
- Convulsions

## Assessment Steps

1. What is the outcome for the woman?
2. Death Information - Woman
3. Date of death
4. What was the place of death?
5. Did the woman deliver any babies before she died?
6. Delivery Outcome
7. How many babies were delivered?
8. How many babies are alive?
9. Date of delivery
10. Where did delivery take place?
11. Please specify other
12. How did she deliver?
13. Who conducted the delivery?
14. Death Information - Baby
15. Place of death
16. Was this a stillbirth?
17. Health Facility ID
18. Baby's Condition
19. What is the condition of baby?
20. Birth weight
21. Birth length
22. What vaccines have they received?
23. Is the child exclusively breastfeeding?
24. Were they initiated on breastfeeding within on hour of delivery?
25. Infected umbilical cord
26. Convulsions
27. Difficulty feeding or drinking
28. Vomits everything
29. Drowsy or unconscious
30. Body stiffness
31. Yellow skin color
32. Blue skin color (hypothermia)
33. Safe Postnatal Practices
34. Lactating mothers need to eat more than usual to feed their baby well. Ask the woman to eat a variety of foods, especially extra fluids, fruits, and vegetables.
35. For the first 6 months, give the baby breastmilk only.
36. To keep the baby warm, use kangaroo mother care, wrap the baby with clothes and cover the head, and keep the baby in a warm room.
37. Always sleep with the baby under a long-lasting insecticide treated net (LLIN).
38. Keep the baby's umbilical cord clean and dry.
39. Which PNC visits have taken place so far?
40. Any additional PNC visits, please report the number of visits below to be counted in the total.

## Decision Rule

- If ANY danger sign above is present → **REFER URGENTLY** to health facility
- Accompany the patient if possible
- Complete follow-up within 3 days

## Instructions for Health Worker

- Does the woman have any of the following danger signs?
- After reporting a death, the person's profile will be locked. If reported in error, you may undo the death report from the profile.
- If babies were delivered, you will have the chance to report on them Next >.
- Maximum 300 characters allowed.
- Additional notes about baby's death
- Sections will repeat if multiple babies were delivered. Please fill out one section per baby.
- Please counsel the family and refer the child to the nearest health center where the baby can be immunized.
- Does the baby have any of the following danger signs?
- The WHO recommends PNC visits within 24 hours and on 3 days, 7 days, and 6 weeks from date of delivery.
- To finish, be sure to click the Submit button.

## When to Use This Workflow
- During or immediately after delivery
