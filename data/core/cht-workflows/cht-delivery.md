# CHT Workflow: Delivery report

*Decision support workflow from Community Health Toolkit (CHT)*
*Used by 40,000+ community health workers in 15 countries*

## Assessment Steps

1. CHW Area ID
2. Non family member
3. Was not accompanied
4. Other relative
5. Who accompanied
6. Enter delivery date
7. On the way to the health facility
8. Place of delivery
9. Delivery details
10. How many babies did
11. How many babies are still alive?
12. Delivery outcome
13. Facility ID
14. _Number of babies delivered:
15. Date of delivery
16. _Place of delivery:
17. Delivery Report
18. Tasks will appear when follow up is needed.
19. _Number of live births:
20. Patient Details
21. _Be sure you submit to complete this action._
22. Results/Summary Page
23. SCHW Region
24. _Remind the mother to enroll the child to EID programme._
25. CHW accompaniment
26. Person to visit health facility alone
27. Type of referral
28. Please add the details of newborns who are live births.
29. Babies born
30. Mwamuna wake
31. Sanapelekezedwe
32. Anapelekezedwa ndi ndani kuchipatala?
33. Ikani Tsiku lomwe anachilira
34. Munjira popita kuchipatala
35. Malo omwe anachilira
36. Tsatanetsatane Wakuchira
37. Kodi mai wabadwitsa ana angati?
38. Ndi ana angati amene ali moyo?
39. Zotsatira za Kuchila
40. Kuchuluka kwa ana obadwa:
41. Tsiku lomwe anachilira
42. _Malo omwe anachilira:
43. Lipoti la kuchira
44. Ntchito iyi idzawoneka ku gawo la Zochita pa…..
45. Kalondolondo
46. _Nambala ya ana amene abadwa amoyo:
47. Mbiri ya Munthuyo
48. _Wonetsetsani kuti mwatumiza tsambali kuti mumalize ntchito iyi._
49. Tsamba La Zotsatira
50. _Kumbutsani mayi zomulembetsa mwana mu pologalamu ya EID_.
51. Anapelekezedwa ndi CHW
52. Anapita yekha ku chipatala
53. Mtundu wakutumiza
54. Chonde lembani dzina/maina a ana amene ali moyo

## Instructions for Health Worker

- _Refer the mother and baby to health facility immediately_
- _Tumizani mayi ndi mwana wake kuchipatala mwansanga_

## When to Use This Workflow
- During or immediately after delivery
