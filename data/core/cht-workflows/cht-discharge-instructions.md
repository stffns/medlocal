# CHT Workflow: Discharge Instructions

*Decision support workflow from Community Health Toolkit (CHT)*
*Used by 40,000+ community health workers in 15 countries*

## Assessment Steps

1. Discharge details
2. Discharge instructions
3. Patient details
4. _Be sure you submit to complete this action._
5. Results/Summary Page
6. Tsatanetsatane wotulutsira
7. Malangizo wotulutsira
8. Mbiri ya wodwala
9. _Wonetsetsani kuti mwatumiza tsambali kuti mumalize ntchito iyi._
10. Tsamba la zotsatira

## Instructions for Health Worker

- _Convey the following message to
- Kodi mwapereka malangizo ochokera kwa adotolo kwa ((dzina la odwala))
- _Perekani uthenga wotsatirawu kwa
