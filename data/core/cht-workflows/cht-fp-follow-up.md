# CHT Workflow: Family planning follow up

*Decision support workflow from Community Health Toolkit (CHT)*
*Used by 40,000+ community health workers in 15 countries*

## Assessment Steps

1. Date of expiry
2. _Please counsel
3. _Next appointment date is
4. Injection/Depo-Provera
5. Intrauterine Device (IUCD)
6. Tubal Ligation
7. Which method has
8. When is the next appointment date?
9. Cultural reasons
10. Husband/partner doesn't want her to use FP
11. Other (specify)
12. The woman is pregnant
13. Religious reasons
14. Experiencing side effects
15. Specify other
16. Are there any side effects?
17. FP follow up
18. Tsiku lokutha mphamvu za njira yakulerayo
19. _Tsiku lopitanso kuchipatala ndi
20. _Dziwitsani
21. _Tumizani (dzina) kuchipatala kuti akatengenso njira ya kulera_
22. Njira ya kulera ya jakiseni
23. Njira yolera ya pa nkono
24. Njira yakulera ya koyilo
25. Kutseka kwa mai
26. Ndi njira yanji ya kulera yomwe (( dzina) akutenga?
27. Tsiku lina loti apite ku chipatala ndi liti?
28. Chifukwa chazikhulupiliro za makolo
29. Abambo akunyumba sakufuna kuti azigwiritsa ntchito njira zakulera
30. Fotokozani za zina
31. Mayi ndi woyembekezera
32. Chifukwa cha chipembezo
33. Akuwona zizindikilo zachilendo kamba kogwiritsa ntchito njira za kulera
34. Kodi n'chifukwa chiyani
35. Pali zovuta zodza kamba ka kugwiritsa ntchito njira ya kulerayi?
36. Kalondolondo wa njira zakulera
