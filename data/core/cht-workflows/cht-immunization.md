# CHT Workflow: Immunization follow up

*Decision support workflow from Community Health Toolkit (CHT)*
*Used by 40,000+ community health workers in 15 countries*

## Danger Signs to Check
Ask about EACH of the following. If ANY is present, refer immediately.

- Abdominal distension and not passing stools
- Convulsions
- Crying continuously after all effort failed
- Diarrhoea
- Eye discharge
- Fever
- Jaundice
- None
- Failing to feed
- Skin rash all over the body
- Swelling, redness or pus discharges on the umbilical cord
- None cannot be selected with any other option!
