# CHT Workflow: Pregnancy follow up

*Decision support workflow from Community Health Toolkit (CHT)*
*Used by 40,000+ community health workers in 15 countries*

## Danger Signs to Check
Ask about EACH of the following. If ANY is present, refer immediately.

- Bleeding
- Constipation
- Convulsions
- Fever
- Haemorrhoids
- Headaches, dizziness or blurry vision
- Lower abdominal pain
- Muscle cramps
- Nausea
- None
- Swelling (feet, hands, legs, face)
- Water bag breaks early
- Please educate the client on danger signs using the job aids
- You cannot select none if you have selected a danger sign!

## Decision Rule

- If ANY danger sign above is present → **REFER URGENTLY** to health facility
- Accompany the patient if possible
- Complete follow-up within 3 days
