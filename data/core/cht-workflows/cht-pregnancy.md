# CHT Workflow: Pregnancy registration

*Decision support workflow from Community Health Toolkit (CHT)*
*Used by 40,000+ community health workers in 15 countries*

## Danger Signs to Check
Ask about EACH of the following. If ANY is present, refer immediately.

- Risk Factors
- Is this
- Has
- If "None of the above" selected, cannot select any other option.
- Does
- Select all that apply.
- Are there additional factors that could make this pregnancy high-risk?
- max characters = 100
- If yes, please describe.
- Danger Sign Check
- Vaginal bleeding
- Fits
- Severe abdominal pain
- Severe headache
- Very pale
- Fever
- Reduced or no fetal movements
- Breaking of water
- Getting tired easily
- Swelling of face and hands
- Breathlessness

## Assessment Steps

1. Gestational Age
2. How would you like to report the pregnancy?
3. Please enter the start date of the LMP.
4. Would you like to report the pregnancy in weeks or months?
5. Please enter the expected date of delivery.
6. You selected "No information is known."
7. Why do you want to register
8. ANC Visits at Health Facility (Past)
9. How many times has
10. Please enter the date if you know it.
11. ANC Visits at Health Facility (Scheduled)
12. Safe Pregnancy Practices

## Decision Rule

- If ANY danger sign above is present → **REFER URGENTLY** to health facility
- Accompany the patient if possible
- Complete follow-up within 3 days
