# CHT Workflow: TB screening

*Decision support workflow from Community Health Toolkit (CHT)*
*Used by 40,000+ community health workers in 15 countries*

## Danger Signs to Check
Ask about EACH of the following. If ANY is present, refer immediately.

- Cough for more than 2 weeks
- Fever
- Night sweats
- None
- Unexplained weight loss
- None cannot be selected with any other option!
- Does
- Kutsokomola kopitilira masabata awiri
- Kutentha thupi
- Kutuluka thukuta losamba usiku
- Palibe
- Kutsika sikelo kodabwitsa
- Simungasankhe palibe ngati mwasankha kale china
- Kodi

## Assessment Steps

1. TB screening
2. _Mpelekezeni
3. Kufufuza TB
