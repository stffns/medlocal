# WHO: Chest Pain Assessment for Community Health Workers

## CRITICAL — Chest Pain Can Be Life-Threatening
Any chest pain must be taken seriously. Assess urgency first.

## Danger Signs — REFER IMMEDIATELY
- Crushing/squeezing chest pain lasting >20 minutes
- Pain spreading to left arm, jaw, or back
- Shortness of breath with chest pain
- Sweating, nausea, pale/grey skin
- Dizziness or fainting
- History of heart disease

## Differential — What Could It Be?

### Heart Attack (Acute Coronary Syndrome)
- Crushing central chest pain, may radiate to arm/jaw
- Sweating, nausea, shortness of breath
- Worse with exertion
- **EMERGENCY:** Give aspirin 300 mg (chew), refer URGENTLY
- If available: sublingual nitroglycerin 0.5 mg

### Pneumonia
- Chest pain with cough, fever, fast breathing
- Pain worse with deep breathing
- See pneumonia protocol for treatment

### Asthma/COPD
- Chest tightness with wheezing
- History of asthma or smoking
- See asthma protocol

### Musculoskeletal
- Sharp pain, worse with movement or pressing on chest wall
- No fever, no shortness of breath
- Treat: paracetamol, rest
- NOT dangerous

### Gastric/Heartburn
- Burning pain behind breastbone
- Worse after eating, lying down
- Relieved by antacids
- Treat: antacid, avoid spicy food, elevate head when sleeping

### Pulmonary Embolism — EMERGENCY
- Sudden chest pain + shortness of breath
- Recent surgery, long travel, or leg swelling
- **REFER URGENTLY**

## In Women — Important
Heart attack symptoms may be different:
- Unusual fatigue, nausea, jaw/back pain
- May NOT have classic crushing chest pain
- Take seriously even if presentation is atypical

## Pre-Referral Actions for Suspected Heart Attack
1. Keep patient calm and seated (or semi-reclined)
2. Give **aspirin 300 mg** — chew, do not swallow whole
3. Loosen tight clothing
4. If oxygen available: give oxygen
5. Do NOT give anything to eat or drink (in case surgery needed)
6. **REFER URGENTLY** — time is critical

Source: WHO PEN for Primary Care; WHO Basic Emergency Care (2018); WHO HEARTS Technical Package
