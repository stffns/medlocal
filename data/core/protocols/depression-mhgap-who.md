# WHO: Depression Management for Community Health Workers (mhGAP)

## When to Suspect Depression
Person has MOST of these symptoms for ≥2 weeks:
- Persistent sadness or low mood most of the day
- Loss of interest or pleasure in usual activities
- Fatigue or low energy
- Sleep problems (too much or too little)
- Appetite changes (eating more or less than usual)
- Difficulty concentrating or making decisions
- Feelings of worthlessness or excessive guilt
- Reduced activity, social withdrawal

## Assessment — Always Ask About Suicide
Ask directly: "Have you had thoughts of hurting yourself or ending your life?"
- This does NOT increase risk — it shows care and opens conversation
- If YES: assess immediate danger (plan, means, timeline)
- If imminent risk: **DO NOT LEAVE ALONE** — remove means of self-harm — **REFER URGENTLY**

## Classification
- **Mild depression:** 2-3 symptoms, can still function daily
- **Moderate depression:** 4+ symptoms, difficulty functioning
- **Severe depression:** Most symptoms + cannot function + may have psychotic features or suicidal thoughts

## Treatment

### Mild to Moderate Depression — Psychosocial Support
1. **Active listening:** Let person talk without judging
2. **Problem-solving counseling:** Help identify practical steps for current problems
3. **Behavioral activation:** Encourage return to enjoyable and social activities, even small ones
4. **Regular physical activity:** 30 minutes most days
5. **Sleep hygiene:** Regular sleep schedule, no stimulants before bed
6. **Social support:** Reconnect with family, friends, community
7. Follow up every 2 weeks

### Moderate to Severe Depression — Medication
- **Fluoxetine** 20 mg once daily in the morning
- Takes 4-6 weeks for full effect — counsel patience
- Continue for at least 9-12 months after improvement
- Do NOT stop suddenly — reduce gradually over 4 weeks
- Common side effects: nausea, headache, insomnia (usually temporary)

### In Pregnancy/Breastfeeding
- Psychosocial support first
- If medication needed: fluoxetine (relatively safe, discuss risks/benefits)
- Screen all pregnant/postnatal women for depression

## Danger Signs — Refer Immediately
- Active suicidal plan with means available
- Psychotic symptoms (hallucinations, delusions)
- Severe self-neglect (not eating, not drinking)
- Manic episode (extremely elevated mood, not sleeping, reckless behavior) — may be bipolar disorder
- Not improving after 8 weeks of treatment

Source: WHO mhGAP Intervention Guide, Version 2.0 (2016, updated 2023); WHO Thinking Healthy Programme
