# WHO/IMCI: Diarrhea Management in Children Under 5

**Classification.**
**No Dehydration:**
- No signs of dehydration
- Child drinks normally, alert, eyes normal, skin pinch goes back quickly

**Some Dehydration (2 or more signs):**
- Restless, irritable
- Sunken eyes
- Drinks eagerly, thirsty
- Skin pinch goes back slowly

**Severe Dehydration (2 or more signs):**
- Lethargic or unconscious
- Sunken eyes
- Not able to drink or drinking poorly
- Skin pinch goes back very slowly (≥2 seconds)

**Treatment.**
**Plan A — No Dehydration (treat at home):**
1. Give extra fluids: ORS solution, clean water, or breastmilk after each loose stool
2. Continue feeding — breastmilk, regular diet
3. Zinc supplementation:
   - Under 6 months: 10 mg/day for 10-14 days
   - 6 months to 5 years: 20 mg/day for 10-14 days
4. Return if: blood in stool, drinking poorly, fever, not getting better in 3 days

**Plan B — Some Dehydration (treat at health facility):**
1. Give ORS solution over 4 hours:
   - Weight <6 kg: 200-400 mL
   - Weight 6-10 kg: 400-700 mL
   - Weight 10-12 kg: 700-900 mL
   - Weight 12-19 kg: 900-1400 mL
   - Or calculate: 75 mL/kg body weight
2. Give small frequent sips from cup
3. If vomiting, wait 10 minutes then continue more slowly
4. Continue breastfeeding
5. After 4 hours: reassess, reclassify, select treatment plan
6. Zinc supplementation as in Plan A

**Plan C — Severe Dehydration (URGENT):**
1. **REFER URGENTLY** to hospital
2. If IV available: Ringer's Lactate or Normal Saline
   - 30 mL/kg in first 30 minutes (infant: first hour)
   - Then 70 mL/kg over next 2.5 hours (infant: 5 hours)
3. If IV not available: give ORS by nasogastric tube — 20 mL/kg/hour for 6 hours
4. Reassess every 1-2 hours
5. Begin zinc when child can drink

**Danger Signs — Refer Immediately.**
- Unable to drink or breastfeed
- Vomits everything
- Convulsions
- Lethargic or unconscious
- Blood in stool (possible dysentery)

**Dysentery (bloody diarrhea).**
1. Give ciprofloxacin 15 mg/kg twice daily for 3 days
2. Follow up in 2 days
3. If not improving after 2 days, refer

**Persistent Diarrhea (≥14 days).**
1. If dehydrated: treat dehydration first (Plan B or C)
2. Refer for assessment if:
   - Blood in stool
   - Under 6 months
3. Give multivitamin and mineral supplements including zinc
4. Follow up in 5 days

Source: WHO/UNICEF IMCI Guidelines — Integrated Management of Childhood Illness
