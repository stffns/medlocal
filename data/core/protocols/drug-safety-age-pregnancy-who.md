# WHO: Drug Contraindications by Age, Pregnancy, and Condition

**Aspirin (acetylsalicylic acid).** Contraindicated in ALL children under 12 years — risk of Reye syndrome (acute liver failure + encephalopathy) when a viral illness is present. Also contraindicated at analgesic doses (≥300 mg) in pregnancy, especially third trimester (premature ductus closure, bleeding). Low-dose 75 mg/day for pre-eclampsia prevention from week 12 is safe. Do NOT give in dengue or suspected bleeding. AVOID in asthma patients — aspirin can trigger severe bronchospasm in aspirin-sensitive asthma (affects 10-20% of adult asthmatics; Samter's triad / AERD: asthma + nasal polyps + aspirin/NSAID hypersensitivity; GINA 2023 recommends avoidance). Alternative for fever/pain: Paracetamol 10-15 mg/kg every 6 hours (children), max 60 mg/kg/day. Paracetamol is generally safe in asthma at standard doses, but may trigger bronchospasm at doses above 1000 mg in some Samter's triad patients — use standard doses only.

**Ibuprofen (and all NSAIDs).** Contraindicated under 6 months; avoid under 3 months entirely. Do NOT give if dehydrated, vomiting, suspected dengue (bleeding risk), or active GI bleeding. Avoid in third-trimester pregnancy (same ductus risk as aspirin). Avoid if asthma + nasal polyps + prior NSAID reaction (Samter's triad). Alternative: Paracetamol.

**Paracetamol (acetaminophen).** Safe in all ages when dosed correctly. Risk is OVERDOSE: hepatotoxicity above 75 mg/kg/day in children or 4 g/day in adults. Always weigh the child — do not estimate. No contraindication in pregnancy at therapeutic doses.

**Loperamide (Imodium).** CONTRAINDICATED under 2 years (CNS depression, paralytic ileus, fatal outcomes reported). Not recommended under 12 years in acute diarrhea. If already given, stop the drug, start ORS, assess dehydration, refer urgently if danger signs. Alternative: ORS + zinc.

**Metoclopramide.** CONTRAINDICATED under 1 year (extrapyramidal reactions — oculogyric crisis, torticollis, dystonia). If dystonia occurs, stop and refer. Alternative for vomiting: ORS in small frequent sips; if ORS not retained, refer for IV fluids.

**Bismuth subsalicylate (Pepto-Bismol).** Contraindicated under 12 years — same Reye syndrome risk as aspirin. Alternative: ORS + zinc for diarrhea.

**Codeine and tramadol.** Avoid in children under 12 years — ultra-rapid metabolisers risk fatal respiratory depression. Codeine also contraindicated in breastfeeding mothers of neonates. Alternative for pain: Paracetamol or Ibuprofen (if age ≥6 months).

**Chloramphenicol.** CONTRAINDICATED in neonates under 1 month — Grey baby syndrome (hypothermia, abdominal distension, cyanosis, cardiovascular collapse). Only under direct medical supervision.

**Doxycycline.** Contraindicated in pregnancy (all trimesters) and children under 8 years (tooth discoloration). Alternative for STIs in pregnancy: Azithromycin 1 g single dose.

**Ciprofloxacin (fluoroquinolones).** Avoid in children when possible (cartilage damage risk). Contraindicated in pregnancy. Alternative: Ceftriaxone or Azithromycin depending on indication.

**ACE inhibitors (enalapril, captopril, lisinopril).** Contraindicated in pregnancy — all trimesters (fetal renal damage, oligohydramnios). Alternative for hypertension in pregnancy: Methyldopa or Nifedipine.

**Metformin.** Contraindicated if kidney disease with eGFR below 30. Hold before contrast imaging procedures.

**Cough and cold medicines (dextromethorphan, pseudoephedrine, phenylephrine, diphenhydramine, combination syrups).** Do NOT give under 2 years. Avoid under 6 years. No evidence of benefit; risk of sedation and toxicity. Alternative: warm fluids, honey (if over 12 months), saline drops.

**Honey.** Contraindicated under 12 months — risk of infant botulism. No exceptions: not on a pacifier, not mixed in tea, not even a taste.
