# WHO: HIV/AIDS Management for Community Health Workers

## When to Suspect HIV
- Persistent fever >1 month
- Unexplained weight loss >10% body weight
- Chronic diarrhea >1 month
- Persistent cough >1 month (in HIV-prevalent areas)
- Recurrent oral thrush
- Herpes zoster (shingles) in a young person
- Recurrent severe bacterial infections in a child

## Testing
- Rapid Diagnostic Test (RDT) — result in 15-20 minutes
- All pregnant women should be tested at first antenatal visit
- Test sexual partners of HIV-positive individuals
- Re-test in 3 months if initial test negative but high risk

## Antiretroviral Treatment (ART)

### Who Should Start ART
- ALL people living with HIV, regardless of CD4 count or clinical stage
- Start as soon as possible after diagnosis — same day if ready
- Priority: pregnant/breastfeeding women, children <5 years, severe symptoms

### First-Line ART (Adults and Adolescents)
- **Preferred:** Dolutegravir (DTG) + Tenofovir (TDF) + Lamivudine (3TC)
- Fixed-dose combination: 1 tablet once daily
- Take at the same time every day, with or without food

### First-Line ART (Children)
- Under 20 kg: Dolutegravir dispersible tablets + ABC + 3TC (weight-based dosing)
- 20 kg and above: Adult DTG-based regimen

### Adherence
- >95% adherence required (miss no more than 1 dose per month)
- Use pill boxes, phone reminders, treatment buddy
- If dose missed: take as soon as remembered (if <12 hours late)
- NEVER stop ART without medical advice — causes drug resistance

## Prevention of Mother-to-Child Transmission (PMTCT)
1. Test all pregnant women for HIV at first antenatal visit
2. If positive: start ART immediately (if not already on ART)
3. Continue ART during pregnancy, delivery, and breastfeeding
4. Infant prophylaxis: Nevirapine syrup for 6 weeks after birth
5. Test infant at 6 weeks (PCR test) and at 18 months (antibody test)
6. Exclusive breastfeeding for 6 months (safer than mixed feeding)

## Common Side Effects of ART
- Nausea, headache, fatigue — usually resolve in 2-4 weeks
- **Dolutegravir:** may cause insomnia, weight gain
- **Tenofovir:** monitor kidney function
- Do NOT stop ART due to mild side effects — counsel and follow up

## Opportunistic Infections — Prevent and Treat
- **Cotrimoxazole prophylaxis:** 1 tablet daily for all HIV+ patients with CD4 <350
- **TB screening:** Ask about cough, fever, weight loss, night sweats at every visit
- **If TB suspected:** refer for sputum test — do NOT delay ART

## Danger Signs — Refer Immediately
- Severe headache with stiff neck (meningitis)
- Difficulty breathing at rest
- Confusion or altered consciousness
- Severe diarrhea with dehydration
- Inability to eat or drink
- New skin rash after starting ART (possible drug reaction)
- Jaundice (yellow eyes) after starting ART

## Counseling Key Points
- HIV is treatable — people on ART live normal lives
- ART is for life — do not stop when feeling better
- Disclose to partner — support partner testing
- Use condoms to prevent transmission and re-infection
- HIV is NOT transmitted by casual contact, sharing food, or mosquitoes

Source: WHO Consolidated Guidelines on HIV Prevention, Testing, Treatment (2021, updated 2025); WHO Recommendations on HIV Clinical Management (2026)
