# WHO: Immunization Schedule for Community Health Workers

## Routine Childhood Immunization Schedule

| Vaccine | Disease | Route |
|---------|---------|-------|
| BCG | Tuberculosis | Intradermal, left upper arm |
| OPV-0 | Polio (zero dose) | Oral, 2 drops |
| Hepatitis B (birth dose) | Hepatitis B | IM, anterolateral thigh |

## Tetanus Toxoid for Pregnant Women
- TT1: At first antenatal contact
- TT2: At least 4 weeks after TT1
- TT3: At least 6 months after TT2 (or next pregnancy)
- TT4: At least 1 year after TT3
- TT5: At least 1 year after TT4
- 5 doses = lifelong protection

## Cold Chain Rules
- Vaccines MUST be stored at 2-8°C (refrigerator, not freezer)
- NEVER freeze DPT, Pentavalent, Hepatitis B, PCV, IPV, or Tetanus (destroys them)
- OPV and Measles CAN be frozen
- Check Vaccine Vial Monitor (VVM) — if the inner square is darker than the outer circle, discard
- Keep vaccines in cold box with ice packs during outreach sessions
- Return unused vaccines to refrigerator within 6 hours

## Injection Safety
- Use auto-disable (AD) syringes — they lock after one use
- New needle and syringe for EVERY injection
- Clean injection site with clean water (alcohol not required)
- Dispose of used needles in sharps container — NEVER recap needles

## Common Side Effects (normal)
- Pain, redness, or swelling at injection site (1-2 days)
- Mild fever (especially after DPT)
- Fussiness in babies (1-2 days)
- Counsel parents: these are NORMAL signs that the vaccine is working

## Adverse Events — Refer
- High fever >40°C after vaccination
- Convulsions within 3 days of DPT
- Severe allergic reaction: difficulty breathing, swelling of face/throat, collapse
- Abscess at injection site (sign of contamination)
- Any reaction within 30 minutes of vaccination → treat as anaphylaxis if severe

## Anaphylaxis Management (at vaccination site)
1. Give IM epinephrine (adrenaline):
   - Child <6 months: 0.05 mL of 1:1000
   - Child 6-12 months: 0.1 mL of 1:1000
   - Child 1-5 years: 0.15 mL of 1:1000
   - Adult: 0.5 mL of 1:1000
2. Place on back, elevate legs
3. Give oxygen if available
4. **REFER URGENTLY** to hospital
5. May repeat epinephrine after 5-15 minutes if no improvement

## Defaulter Tracking
- Keep immunization register updated
- Home visit within 1 week if child misses appointment
- Never restart vaccination series — just continue from where stopped
- Any dose given counts, regardless of interval

Source: WHO Immunization in Practice Module (2015, updated 2023); WHO Position Papers on Vaccines
