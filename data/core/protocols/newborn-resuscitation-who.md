# WHO: Newborn Resuscitation — Baby Not Breathing at Birth

**When Baby is NOT Breathing or NOT Crying After Birth.**
**Step-by-step Actions (do within first 60 seconds — "The Golden Minute"):**
1. **Clamp and cut the cord immediately** — do not wait
2. **Move baby to a warm, flat surface** — keep baby warm
3. **Clear the airway:**
   - Position head in neutral position (not bent forward or back)
   - Suction mouth first, then nose (only if needed)
4. **Stimulate breathing:**
   - Rub the back firmly 2-3 times
   - Flick the soles of the feet

**If Baby is STILL Not Breathing After Stimulation:**
5. **Begin ventilation with bag and mask immediately:**
   - Use correct mask size (covers mouth and nose, not eyes)
   - Rate: 40-60 breaths per minute (about 1 squeeze per second)
   - Watch for chest rise with each breath
   - If chest does NOT rise: reposition head, check mask seal, suction again

6. **Ventilate for 1 minute, then reassess:**
   - Is baby breathing? → Stop ventilation, continue care
   - Heart rate present but no breathing? → Continue ventilation
   - No heart rate, no breathing? → Begin chest compressions (3 compressions : 1 ventilation)

**When to REFER URGENTLY:**
- No improvement after 1 minute of effective ventilation
- Baby gasping (irregular, ineffective breaths)
- Baby remains blue or pale despite ventilation
- Baby is floppy with no response

**What NOT To Do.**
- Do NOT hold baby upside down
- Do NOT slap the baby
- Do NOT squeeze the chest
- Do NOT put anything in the baby's mouth
- Do NOT blow into the baby's face

**After Successful Resuscitation.**
1. Place baby skin-to-skin on mother's chest
2. Keep warm — cover both mother and baby
3. Monitor breathing closely for next 2 hours
4. Initiate breastfeeding when baby is ready
5. Record what happened and for how long

Source: WHO Guidelines on Basic Newborn Resuscitation (2012, reaffirmed 2022); Helping Babies Breathe (HBB) program
