# WHO: Oral Health for Community Health Workers

## Toothache — Assessment and Treatment

### Simple Toothache (no swelling, no fever)
- Clean around the tooth, rinse with warm salt water
- **Paracetamol** or **ibuprofen** for pain
- Clove oil on cotton ball applied to tooth (natural analgesic)
- **Refer** to dental care when available

### Dental Abscess (swelling, pain, possible fever)
- Swollen face or gum near painful tooth
- May have pus draining
- **Treatment:**
  - **Amoxicillin** 500 mg three times daily for 5 days
  - PLUS **Metronidazole** 400 mg three times daily for 5 days
  - Paracetamol/ibuprofen for pain
  - Warm salt water rinses
  - **Refer** for drainage or extraction
- If severe swelling spreading to neck or floor of mouth: **REFER URGENTLY** (Ludwig's angina — can block airway)

### Knocked-Out Tooth (avulsion)
- Handle tooth by crown only (not root)
- If clean: reimplant immediately, hold in place
- If dirty: rinse gently with milk or saline, reimplant
- If cannot reimplant: store in milk or under tongue (keep moist)
- **REFER immediately** to dentist — best results within 30 minutes

## Mouth Sores

### Oral Thrush (Candida)
- White patches inside mouth, painful
- Common in babies, HIV+ patients, patients on antibiotics
- **Treatment:** Nystatin suspension 100,000 IU 4x daily for 7 days
- In adults with recurrent thrush: **offer HIV test**

### Mouth Ulcers (Aphthous)
- Small painful ulcers inside cheeks, lips, or tongue
- Usually resolve in 7-10 days
- Rinse with warm salt water
- Avoid spicy/acidic foods
- If not healing in 2 weeks: **refer** (rule out cancer)

## Gum Disease
- Red, swollen, bleeding gums
- Bad breath
- Teeth becoming loose
- **Treatment:** Improved brushing, salt water rinses
- **Refer** for scaling if severe

## Prevention — Key Messages
- Brush teeth twice daily with fluoride toothpaste
- If no toothbrush: use clean cloth or chewing stick
- Limit sugar — especially between meals
- Do not put sugar, honey, or sweet drinks in baby bottles
- First dental visit by age 1 or when first tooth appears
- Do not share toothbrushes

## NOMA (Cancrum Oris) — EMERGENCY
- Severe destructive infection of mouth and face
- Occurs in malnourished children with poor oral hygiene
- Starts as gum ulcer → rapidly destroys tissue → facial disfigurement
- **REFER URGENTLY**
- Treatment: antibiotics (amoxicillin + metronidazole), nutritional support, wound care

Source: WHO Oral Health Guidelines; MSF Clinical Guidelines; WHO Essential Medicines List
