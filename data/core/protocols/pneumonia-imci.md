# WHO/IMCI: Pneumonia Management in Children Under 5

**Assessment — Count Breaths Per Minute.**
Count for a full 60 seconds when the child is calm.

**Fast Breathing Thresholds:**
- Under 2 months: ≥60 breaths/minute
- 2-11 months: ≥50 breaths/minute
- 12 months to 5 years: ≥40 breaths/minute

**Classification.**
**No Pneumonia (cough or cold):**
- No fast breathing
- No chest indrawing
- No danger signs

**Pneumonia:**
- Fast breathing for age AND/OR
- Chest indrawing (lower chest wall goes in when breathing in)

**Severe Pneumonia or Very Severe Disease:**
- Any danger sign:
  - Unable to drink or breastfeed
  - Vomits everything
  - Convulsions
  - Lethargic or unconscious
  - Stridor in a calm child
  - Severe chest indrawing

**Treatment.**
**No Pneumonia (cough or cold):**
1. No antibiotic needed
2. Safe remedy for cough: warm fluids, breastmilk
3. Advise caregiver when to return (if breathing becomes fast, difficulty breathing, unable to drink)

**Pneumonia (treat at home):**
1. **Oral amoxicillin** for 5 days:
   - 2-12 months (4-10 kg): 250 mg twice daily
   - 12 months to 3 years (10-14 kg): 500 mg twice daily
   - 3-5 years (14-19 kg): 750 mg twice daily
2. Follow up in 3 days
3. If worse or not improving: refer

**Severe Pneumonia — URGENT:**
1. **REFER URGENTLY** to hospital
2. Give first dose of antibiotic before referral:
   - Oral amoxicillin (as above) if injection not available
   - OR IM ampicillin 50 mg/kg + IM gentamicin 7.5 mg/kg
3. If wheezing: give inhaled salbutamol (bronchodilator) before referral
4. Keep child warm
5. If oxygen available: give oxygen

**Pneumonia in Young Infants (Under 2 Months).**
- ANY fast breathing (≥60/min) or severe chest indrawing = **REFER URGENTLY**
- Pre-referral: IM ampicillin 50 mg/kg + IM gentamicin 7.5 mg/kg
- Keep warm, breastfeed frequently

**Weight-only queries — safety floor.**
If the CHW reports only the weight of a child with pneumonia and the child weighs **≤5 kg**, treat as a young infant (<2 months) until proven otherwise: **REFER URGENTLY**, give IM ampicillin 50 mg/kg + IM gentamicin 7.5 mg/kg before referral, do **NOT** start outpatient oral amoxicillin. A healthy 2-month-old at the 50th percentile weighs ~5 kg, so ≤5 kg without a known age implies either a young infant or severe malnutrition — both require referral. If only weight is known and weight is 4-10 kg but the child is confirmed ≥2 months and not malnourished, the standard oral amoxicillin band (250 mg twice daily) applies.

**Danger Signs — Refer Immediately.**
- Unable to drink or breastfeed
- Vomits everything
- Convulsions
- Lethargic or unconscious
- Stridor when calm
- Severe malnutrition

**Follow-up at Day 3.**
- If breathing slower, less fever, eating better → complete 5 days of amoxicillin
- If same or worse → refer to hospital
- If new danger sign → refer URGENTLY

Source: WHO/UNICEF IMCI Guidelines — Integrated Management of Childhood Illness
