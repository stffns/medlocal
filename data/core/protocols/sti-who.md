# WHO: Sexually Transmitted Infections (STIs) for Community Health Workers

## BEFORE Treating Any STI — Mandatory Assessment
1. **Ask about drug allergies** — especially penicillin, cephalosporins, sulfa drugs
2. **Ask about pregnancy** — some antibiotics are contraindicated
3. **Confirm key symptoms** — do not treat based on one symptom alone
4. **Ask about sexual history** — number of partners, condom use, partner symptoms
5. **Offer HIV test** — STIs increase HIV risk

## Syndromic Management — Treat Based on Symptoms

### Urethral Discharge (men)
- Symptoms: discharge from penis, pain when urinating
- Treat for BOTH gonorrhea + chlamydia:
  - Ceftriaxone 250 mg IM single dose (gonorrhea)
  - PLUS Azithromycin 1g oral single dose (chlamydia)
- Treat all sexual partners
- Abstain from sex until treatment complete + 7 days

### Vaginal Discharge
- Symptoms: abnormal discharge, itching, odor, pain
- If cervical infection risk (young, multiple partners, partner with discharge):
  - Treat for gonorrhea + chlamydia (as above)
- If vaginitis (white curdy = candida, fishy odor = bacterial vaginosis):
  - Metronidazole 2g oral single dose (BV/trichomoniasis)
  - Clotrimazole pessary 500 mg single dose (candida)

### Genital Ulcer
- Symptoms: sore/ulcer on genitals
- Treat for BOTH syphilis + chancroid:
  - Benzathine penicillin 2.4 million units IM single dose (syphilis)
  - PLUS Azithromycin 1g oral single dose (chancroid)
- If herpes suspected (painful, recurrent, vesicles): Acyclovir 400 mg 3x daily for 7 days
- **Offer HIV test** — genital ulcers increase HIV risk

### Lower Abdominal Pain in Women — Suspect Pelvic Inflammatory Disease (PID)
- Confirm BEFORE treating:
  - Vaginal discharge present?
  - Pain with cervical movement on exam?
  - Sexual history — multiple partners, unprotected sex?
  - Rule out pregnancy (ectopic pregnancy is a surgical emergency)
  - Ask about drug allergies (3 antibiotics will be given)
- Treatment (only after assessment confirms PID):
  - Ceftriaxone 250 mg IM single dose
  - PLUS Doxycycline 100 mg twice daily for 14 days
  - PLUS Metronidazole 500 mg twice daily for 14 days
- If severe (fever >=38C, rebound tenderness, unable to eat): **REFER URGENTLY**
- Treat sexual partners
- Follow up in 3 days — if not improving, REFER

## Syphilis in Pregnancy
- Screen ALL pregnant women at first antenatal visit
- If positive: Benzathine penicillin 2.4 million units IM
  - Early syphilis: single dose
  - Late/unknown duration: 3 doses, 1 week apart
- Untreated syphilis causes stillbirth, neonatal death, congenital syphilis

## Partner Notification
- All sexual partners in last 60 days must be treated
- Provide partner notification slip
- Treat partners even if they have no symptoms

## Condom Promotion
- Condoms prevent all STIs including HIV
- Demonstrate correct use
- Provide condoms at every visit

## Danger Signs — Refer Immediately
- Severe lower abdominal pain with fever (ruptured abscess)
- Genital ulcer with swollen inguinal lymph nodes draining pus
- Pregnancy with positive syphilis (urgent treatment needed)
- Symptoms not improving after 7 days of treatment

Source: WHO Guidelines for the Management of STIs (2021); WHO Syndromic Management Guidelines
